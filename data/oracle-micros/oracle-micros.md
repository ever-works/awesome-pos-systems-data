## Overview

Oracle MICROS is a hospitality and retail point-of-sale platform owned by Oracle Corporation. It serves restaurants, hotels, casinos, and retail operations with integrated POS hardware and software.

## Features

- Point of sale terminals
- Kitchen display systems
- Table and reservation management
- Inventory and stock control
- Reporting and analytics
- Integration with Oracle Hospitality suite

## Deployment

Cloud and on-premise

## Pricing

Commercial paid enterprise software; pricing available via Oracle sales.
