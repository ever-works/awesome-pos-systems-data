## Overview

lsFusion is a free, open-source, self-hosted ERP and CRM platform for small businesses. It unifies inventory, invoicing, manufacturing, sales, projects, HR, and point-of-sale functionality in a single system.

## Features

- Point-of-sale functionality for retail operations
- Inventory management and stock tracking
- Invoicing and billing
- Manufacturing and production management
- Sales and CRM modules
- Project management
- Human resources management
- Self-hosted deployment
- Open-source platform

## Pricing

Free and open source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted.
