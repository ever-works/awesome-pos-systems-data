## Overview

Adyen is a global payment company that provides in-person and online payment processing. Its Terminal solution enables merchants to accept card-present payments through payment terminals connected to Adyen's platform.

## Features

- Credit, debit, and prepaid card acceptance
- Contactless and NFC payments
- Apple Pay, Google Pay support
- Unified commerce (online and in-person)
- APIs and SDKs for custom integrations
- Real-time transaction reporting

## Deployment

Cloud-based with countertop and mobile hardware

## Pricing


Interchange-plus pricing model with transaction fees; no monthly software fees.
