## Overview

PAR Technology Corporation is a global provider of point-of-sale and back-office systems for the restaurant and hospitality industry. Its Brink POS platform is a cloud-based restaurant management solution.

## Features

- Brink POS cloud platform
- Kitchen display systems
- Online ordering integration
- Guest management and loyalty
- Inventory and labor management
- Reporting and analytics

## Deployment

Cloud and on-premise

## Pricing

Commercial paid software; pricing available via PAR Technology sales.
