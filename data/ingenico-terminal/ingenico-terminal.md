## Overview

Ingenico Group (now part of Worldline) manufactures point-of-sale payment terminals and payment processing hardware used by merchants worldwide for in-person transactions.

## Features

- Credit and debit card terminals
- Contactless and NFC payments
- Mobile payment acceptance
- EMV chip card support
- PIN entry and signature capture
- Ethernet, Wi-Fi, and 4G connectivity

## Deployment

Hardware terminals

## Pricing

Commercial paid hardware and payment processing services; pricing varies by region and volume.
