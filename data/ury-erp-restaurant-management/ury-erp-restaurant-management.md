## Overview

URY is a FOSS restaurant management system super powered by ERPNext. It extends ERPNext with restaurant-focused capabilities for order management, table handling, and restaurant operations.

## Deployment

Self-hosted, built on the ERPNext platform (Frappe framework).

## Technology

TypeScript is the primary language used in this repository.
