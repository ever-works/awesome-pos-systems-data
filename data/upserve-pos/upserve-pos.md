## Overview

Upserve POS is a restaurant point-of-sale and management system designed for restaurants of various sizes, from startups to enterprises.

## Features

- Customer Management
- Offline Mode
- Employee Management
- Split Billing
- Tableside Ordering
- Sales Reporting
- Real Time
- Online Ordering

## Pricing

- Core: $99 per location per month
- Pro: $249 per location per month

## Deployment

Cloud-based SaaS deployment.
