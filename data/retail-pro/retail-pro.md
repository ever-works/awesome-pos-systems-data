## Overview

Retail Pro is point-of-sale and retail management software designed for independent and multi-store retailers. It provides sales processing, inventory control, and customer relationship management tools.

## Features

- Point of sale transactions
- Inventory and stock management
- Customer database and loyalty
- Employee management and commissions
- Purchasing and receiving
- Multi-store and matrix inventory
- Reporting and analytics

## Deployment

Cloud and on-premise

## Pricing

Commercial paid software; licensing fees vary by module and store count.
