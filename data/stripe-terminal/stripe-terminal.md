## Overview

Stripe Terminal is Stripe's in-person payments product, allowing businesses to build custom point-of-sale experiences and accept physical card payments using Stripe's backend infrastructure.

## Features

- Pre-certified card readers (Stripe Reader S700, BBPOS WisePOS E, Verifone P400)
- Tap to Pay on iPhone and Android
- Custom POS app development via SDKs
- Cloud-based device management
- Online and offline payment modes
- Receipt printing and inventory sync

## Deployment

Cloud-based with mobile and countertop hardware

## Pricing

Pay-as-you-go transaction fees plus hardware costs; no monthly fees for the software platform.
