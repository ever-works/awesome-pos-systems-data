## Overview

Verifone is a global provider of point-of-sale payment terminals and commerce solutions. Its devices are used by merchants to accept card, contactless, and mobile payments in-store and on the go.

## Features

- Credit and debit card acceptance
- Contactless NFC payments
- Apple Pay, Google Pay, Samsung Pay support
- EMV chip card processing
- PIN entry and receipt printing
- Wi-Fi, Ethernet, Bluetooth, and 4G connectivity

## Deployment

Hardware terminals

## Pricing

Commercial paid hardware and payment processing services; pricing varies by region and volume.
