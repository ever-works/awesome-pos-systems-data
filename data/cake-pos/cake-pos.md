## Overview

CAKE POS is a cloud-based point-of-sale system built for independent restaurants and bars. It streamlines ordering, payments, and table management while offering online ordering, guest engagement tools, and real-time reporting, helping owners speed up service, reduce errors, and grow revenue efficiently.

## Key Features

- Split Billing
- Payment Management
- Inventory Management
- Offline Mode
- Employee Management
- Real-time reporting
- Multi-location support
- Reservations Management
- Online ordering
- Guest engagement tools
- Order management
