## Overview

GOFRUGAL Restaurant POS is a restaurant billing software with inventory and kitchen order-taking solution for restaurants, bars, and fine dining. It is a simple restaurant management system to manage all transactions, recipe management, table layout and guest seating, and supports online food ordering systems.

## Key Features

- Order Management
- Table Management
- Loyalty Programs
- Kitchen Display System
- Multi-store management
- Mobile POS
- Inventory Management
- Reservations Management
- Recipe management
- Transaction management

## Pricing

- Basic: $6 per store per month
- Elite: $12 per store per month

## Deployment

Cloud-based (SaaS).

## Target Segments

Startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprises including restaurants, bars, and fine dining establishments.
