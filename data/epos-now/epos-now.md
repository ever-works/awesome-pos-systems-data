## Overview

Epos Now is a cloud-based retail POS system that helps businesses streamline their operations. A 30-day free trial is available to evaluate the system's capabilities.

## Pricing

- **Standard**: $39 per month
- **Premium**: $69 per month

## Target Segments

Epos Now is tailored for startups, SMBs, mid-market businesses, and enterprises.

## Features

- Employee Management
- Purchase Orders
- Third Party Integrations
- Promotions And Discounts
- Sales Reporting
- Inventory Management
- Customizable Receipts
- Real Time
