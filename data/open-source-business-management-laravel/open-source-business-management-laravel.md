## Overview

Open-source business management system with ERP, POS, invoicing, and CMS features, built with Laravel and Docker-ready.

## Features

- ERP (Enterprise Resource Planning)
- Point of Sale (POS)
- Invoicing
- CMS (Content Management System)
- Laravel-based
- Docker-ready

## Pricing

Free and open-source.

## Deployment

Self-hosted (Docker-ready).
