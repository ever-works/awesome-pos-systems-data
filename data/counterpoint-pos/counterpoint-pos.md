## Overview

Counterpoint is a retail point-of-sale and business management system originally developed by Radiant Systems and now part of NCR Voyix. It serves specialty retailers, convenience stores, and other small-to-mid-size merchants.

## Features

- Point of sale transactions
- Inventory and purchasing
- Customer loyalty and marketing
- Employee management
- E-commerce integration
- Reporting and analytics

## Deployment

Cloud and on-premise

## Pricing

Commercial paid software; pricing available via NCR Voyix sales.
