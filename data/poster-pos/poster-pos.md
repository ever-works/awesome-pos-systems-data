## Overview

Poster POS is a cloud-based point-of-sale system designed for front-office operations, inventory management, finances, analytics, and customer relationship management. It is positioned as easy to set up and provides online access to business statistics, finances, and inventory data.

## Pricing

- **Startup**: $14 per month
- **Mini**: $24 per month
- **Business**: $34 per month
- **Pro**: $54 per month

## Target Segments

Poster POS is tailored for startups, SMBs, mid-market businesses, and enterprises.

## Features

- Loyalty Programs
- Order Management
- Table Management
- Inventory Management
- Customer Management
- Customizable Receipts
- Online Ordering
- Employee Management
