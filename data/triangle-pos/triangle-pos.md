## Overview

Triangle POS is an open source Inventory Management system with POS functionality, built with Laravel 10, Bootstrap 4, and Livewire 3.

## Features

- Inventory management
- Point of sale system
- Built with Laravel 10 (PHP)
- Bootstrap 4 frontend
- Livewire 3 for reactive interfaces

## Pricing

Completely free to use.

## Deployment

Self-hosted.
