## Overview

uniCenta oPOS is an open-source point of sale application forked from Openbravo POS. It is designed for retail and hospitality environments and can be deployed on-premise.

## Features

- Point of sale transactions
- Inventory management
- Customer and supplier management
- Multi-store support
- Reporting and analytics
- Receipt and barcode printing

## Deployment

Self-hosted / On-premise
