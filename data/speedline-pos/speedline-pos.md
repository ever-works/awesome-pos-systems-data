## Overview

SpeedLine Pizza POS is a point-of-sale system designed for pizza and delivery restaurant operations, offered by SpeedLine Solutions Inc. It is positioned for startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprise customers.

## Features

- Inventory Management
- Delivery Management
- Employee Management
- Order Entry
- Online Ordering
- Menu Management
- Third Party Integrations
- Caller Id
