## Overview

Cegid is a European software publisher providing retail and financial management solutions. Cegid Retail is its point-of-sale and omnichannel commerce platform for fashion and specialty retailers.

## Features

- Point of sale transactions
- Omnichannel commerce (online and in-store)
- Inventory and replenishment
- Customer relationship management
- Mobile POS
- Reporting and analytics

## Deployment

Cloud-based

## Pricing

Commercial paid enterprise software; pricing available via Cegid sales.
