## Overview

Talech is intelligent POS software suited for large business organizations. Beyond managing cash registers, it handles various other aspects of business operations.

## Pricing

- **Starter**: $44 per month
- **Standard**: $62 per month
- **Premium**: $89 per month

## Target Segments

Talech is tailored for startups, SMBs, mid-market businesses, and enterprises.

## Features

- Order Management
- Customer Management
- Barcode Scanning
- Discount Management
- Customizable Receipts
- Employee Management
- Appointment Scheduling
- Inventory Management
