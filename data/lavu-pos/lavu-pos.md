## Overview

Lavu restaurant POS software provides tools for restaurant businesses including inventory tracking, online delivery order management, card payment acceptance, and detailed reporting.

## Features

- Offline Mode
- Customer Management
- Mobile POS
- Third Party Integrations
- Inventory Management
- Menu Management
- Point of Sale (POS)
- Reservations Management

## Pricing

$59 per user per month, billed annually.

## Deployment

Cloud-based deployment.
