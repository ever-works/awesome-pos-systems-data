## Overview

Brink-POS is a cloud-based restaurant POS software designed to meet customer needs, featuring automatic upgrades, access from anywhere, and real-time data views. It simplifies menu rollout and improves business efficiency. It is positioned for startups, SMBs, mid-market, and enterprise customers.

## Features

- Customer Management
- Table Management
- Gift Management
- Third Party Integrations
- Menu Management
- Security Features
- Real Time
- Offline Mode

## Pricing

Starting price: $90 per month.

## Deployment

Cloud-based, accessible from anywhere with automatic upgrades.
